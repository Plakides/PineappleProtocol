The PINEAPPLE Protocol

A computing mystery in eight rooms

Year 9 · 10–15 minutes · ⌨️ + 👆

How to Play

  • 1. Pick your character, your house, and a name.
  • 2. An AI called PINEAPPLE has gone rogue. Travel the school, solve eight puzzles, and shut it down.
  • 3. Each puzzle teaches a real Computer Science idea and unlocks a career card.
  • 4. Tap, click, drag, type. Different puzzles use different controls — instructions appear in each room.
  • 5. No saving. Finish in one sitting (~15 min).
